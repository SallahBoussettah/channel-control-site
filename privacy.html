Privacy Policy

Channel Control · Last updated October 13, 2026

Who uses Channel Control

Channel Control is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to manage the YouTube channels he owns. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

Data accessed through YouTube API Services

Channel Control uses YouTube API Services. When the owner connects one of his channels, it receives permission to:

  • upload videos, set their thumbnails, titles, descriptions and publish times on that channel;
  • read that channel's videos, their privacy and schedule status, and basic channel statistics (subscribers, views).

It does not access any other user's data, comments, messages, subscribers' personal information, or any Google service other than YouTube.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish and schedule the owner's videos and to show the owner which videos are posted, scheduled or due. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or combined with other data.

How the data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computers and servers, readable only by the owner's accounts. Video information is kept only as long as the owner manages the channel, and the owner can delete it at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the tool, or from the Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google policies

By using a channel connected to Channel Control you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
